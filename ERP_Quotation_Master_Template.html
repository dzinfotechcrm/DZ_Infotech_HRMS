01 Project Overview

[1–2 sentence restatement of the client's need, in their own words where possible — proves we listened.]

Module Flow

Purchase → Inventory → Production → Job Work → Sales & Dispatch → HR & Payroll → Reports

Module Summary

  • Purchase — vendor POs, shortage-driven ordering, GRN tracking against open POs.
  • Inventory — multi-location stock, transfers, reorder alerts, physical-count adjustments, valuation.
  • Production — multi-level BOMs, stage-wise routing, job-work tracking, live work-order status.
  • Sales & Dispatch — sales orders, delivery challans, partial dispatch, real-time finished-goods visibility.
  • HR & Payroll — employee master, attendance, leave, payroll and payslip generation.
  • Reports & Access Control — on-screen and exportable reports, role-based access across departments.

02 Detailed Functional Scope

Purchase

  • Material requirement visibility — shortages calculated from active production, current stock, and open POs
  • Purchase Orders raised against a vendor with item, quantity, rate, and delivery date
  • PO status tracking — Open, Partially Received, Closed
  • GRN entry with partial/multiple deliveries and automatic mismatch flags against the PO

Inventory

  • Multi-location stock — raw material, factory floor/WIP, finished goods, and material at job-work vendors
  • Stock transfers between locations, updating both ends in one step
  • Reorder alerts when stock falls below a set level
  • Stock adjustments after physical count, with a logged audit trail
  • Stock valuation by item and location

Production

  • Bills of Materials, including multi-level BOMs and BOM versioning
  • Routing and stage-wise tracking for every Work Order
  • Job-work tracking — material sent to a vendor, outstanding balance, and receipts
  • Work Orders with BOM auto-explosion for material requirement
  • Live production status and completion % without a floor visit

Sales & Dispatch

  • Sales Orders with items, quantities, rates, and delivery dates
  • Dispatch Notes / Delivery Challans, including partial dispatch
  • Real-time finished-goods visibility for delivery commitments
  • Order status tracking — Pending, Partially Dispatched, Fully Dispatched

HR & Payroll

  • Employee master for permanent and daily-wage/contract staff
  • Daily attendance marking, individually or in bulk
  • Leave types and balances tracked automatically per employee
  • Monthly payroll from attendance and salary structure, including daily-wage computation
  • Payslips and a consolidated monthly salary register

Note: this module covers HR & Payroll only. If ledger, GST, TDS, bank reconciliation, or financial-statement functionality is required, it must be listed separately as an Accounting module.

Reports & Access Control

  • On-screen and downloadable (PDF/Excel) reports across all modules
  • Role-based access, assignable and adjustable at any time
